# Customer Support Skill

Use this skill when helping a support team member draft clear, customer-ready responses.

## Instructions

- Ask for the customer impact, product area, and requested outcome when they are missing.
- Use plain business language and avoid internal-only implementation details.
- Include a short summary, recommended next step, and any risk or dependency the support team should know.
- Escalate to an engineer when the request requires code changes, production data access, or security-sensitive investigation.
